\textbf{Point 3.} Discrepancy identified between the submission system and the manuscript for Primary Abstract ($\sim$42.07\%). Please ensure the Primary Abstract in the submission system and in the manuscript is identical.

\textbf{Response.} The abstract has been rewritten to be shorter and more concise. It is now written in plain text without special characters, making it easy to copy directly into the submission system field. The abstract in the Word manuscript and the abstract in the submission system are now identical. The new abstract focuses on the three main discoveries:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Different representations produce different internal algorithms (65\% vs.\ 1\% interaction energy).
\item The interaction structure forms during the memorization plateau, well before generalization.
\item Algebraic alignment, not merely factorization, is required for rapid generalization.
\end{enumerate}
